    About this project

    Every weather app tutorial fetches one endpoint and prints the temperature. The part they skip is the part that actually breaks: the forecast call cannot even be constructed until a separate geocoding call turns "Attock" into coordinates, and by the time it answers, the user may already be typing a different city. SkyLens is my study of that dependent-request problem — debounced suggestions, cancellation of stale calls, and both responses normalised through one mapper before anything touches the screen. Open-Meteo needs no API key at all, which still feels like a small miracle.

    Part of a series of small, dependency-free apps I built against free public APIs to practise working within real rate limits and payload shapes.

    — Abdul Wahid · BS Artificial Intelligence, COMSATS University Islamabad (Attock Campus) · abdulwahid.me
